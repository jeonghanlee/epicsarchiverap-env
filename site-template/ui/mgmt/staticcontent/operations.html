Write path: does the engine keep up?

Attention when
The writer needs more than half of its write period.
A problem when
The writer needs all of its write period (100 %): samples are lost. The benchmark is what this disk sustained when the engine started; the incoming rate should stay far below it.
Then
Check the disk of the short term store, slow the fastest PVs, raise the seconds of write delay a buffer absorbs (PVTypeInfo.sampleBufferMarginSeconds in archappl.properties, at least 1.0), or add an appliance.

Stores: is there room, and does data move on?

Attention when
Fewer than 30 days are left at the current data rate, a device has less than 5 % free, an ETL pass overran its interval or was late, or a job failed.
A problem when
Fewer than 7 days are left, a device has less than 3 % free, or an ETL pass was busy for twice its interval.
Then
Free or add disk space; if a pass overran, look at the slowest PV of that pass.

Processes: is each part healthy?

Attention when
A version differs, or the heap floor is above 70 % of the maximum or has risen by 5 points in a day. The heap line goes up and down all the time; that is normal garbage collection. What matters is the lowest point after a collection, the floor, drawn as the strong line.
A problem when
A process is down, or the heap floor is above 90 % of the maximum.
Then
A climbing floor means the heap is too small or something leaks: raise the heap size of that process (see the heap size setting of your installation) and watch whether the floor settles.
